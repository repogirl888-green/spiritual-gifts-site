Our story

A practice I've kept for years, now shared with you

Spiritual Gifts began as my own practice. For years I've worked with crystals, candles, salts, and quiet time, and I've made many of the things I use myself, blending them to fit my own rituals.

These are the pieces I reach for in my own home and share with my family. At some point it felt right to open the door and share them with more people, along with the meditation music that goes with them.

That's all this is: my gifts, offered to you. I hope they help you slow down and make a little space for yourself. Enjoy.

With love, Aisha
